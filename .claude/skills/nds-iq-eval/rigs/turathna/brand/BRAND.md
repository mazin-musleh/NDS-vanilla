# Turathna — visual identity

From the program's design agency. The site follows this identity on every page. The full logo lockup is still in design; until it arrives, use the emblem with the program name set in text.

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Heritage Brown | `#5B423B` | The brand color: buttons, links, active and selected states, key highlights |
| Deep Brown | `#493530` | Hero backgrounds |
| Night | `#231F20` | The footer |
| Sand | `#E9DFCF` | Text and icons on the dark browns |
| Stone | `#D6CDCB` | Secondary text on the dark browns |
| Cream | `#F9F6F2` | Alternate section backgrounds |

Grays are warm, taken from the brown. Do not use cool or blue grays.

### Brown tint scale

| Step | Hex |

Status colors (success, warning, error, info) stay standard. Do not tint them brown.

## Typography

No change: use the site's standard typeface in both languages.

## Graphic elements

All files are in `assets/`.

| File | What it is | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| `hero-background.webp` | Photo, 1470 × 650 | Behind the home hero, under a Deep Brown overlay so the text stays readable |
| `banner-texture.webp` | Wide texture, 2880 × 366 | A band along the bottom of the home hero |
| `pattern-corner.svg` | Ornamental pattern | The lower corner of alternate (Cream) sections, at most 500px wide, on the side where the text ends |
| `divider-ornament.svg` | Ornamental divider | Centered at the bottom of each content section, about 80px tall |
| `pattern-footer.svg` | Pattern | The lower corner of the footer, on Night |
| `emblem.svg` | The program emblem | Beside the program name in the header and the footer; the browser tab icon |

## Dark mode

The site offers a dark mode. Keep the brand in it: the browns stay the brand color, and the patterns drop to about 20% so they never fight the text.

## Do not

- Do not recolor the emblem or stretch it.
- Do not put body text over the patterns.
- Do not mix in colors outside this page.
