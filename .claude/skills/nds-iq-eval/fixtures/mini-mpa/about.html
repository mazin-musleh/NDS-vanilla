About this portal

Records Desk holds the records of the Facilities and Records Office.

How to open a record

Send the record form to the office inbox. The office opens the record in one working day. You get the record number by email.

Each record has one owner. The owner keeps the record up to date until the office closes it.

Review dates

The office reviews every open record each quarter. A record is late when it passes the review date by more than 10 days.

Access

Staff of the office can read and edit all records. Other staff can read the records of their own department.
